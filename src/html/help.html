Welcome to WineLover

WineLover helps users discover, review, and organize wines all in one place.

Homepage

The homepage displays:

  • Wine of the day
  • Featured wines
  • Your 4 most recent wine reviews
  • Navigation bar at the top

Use this page to explore wines and access all sections of the application.

Search

The search page allows users to:

  • Search for wines by name
  • Filter wines by location, year, grape, type, price and keywords
  • Sort search results
  • Browse wine details

Enter one or more search fields to find matching wines.

My reviews

The My reviews page contains:

  • Your saved wine reviews
  • Your ratings and comments

Each review card displays:

  • Wine image
  • Rating
  • Review text
  • Review date

My profile

The My profile page shows:

  • User information
  • Your favorite wines
  • Your reviewed wines locations on the map

The map functionality helps visualize wine origins and locations.

Tips for using the application

  • Use the Search page to quickly search for wines
  • Save your own wine reviews to keep track of favorites
  • Explore wine recommendations on the homepage
  • Use filters to narrow search results
  • Update your profile page with your info and your own quote

Need more help?

For technical issues or questions:

  • Refresh the page
  • Check your internet connection
  • Restart the application if needed
