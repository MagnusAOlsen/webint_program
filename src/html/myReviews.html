Name of wine

One simply does not drink this wine so much as bear witness to it, a transcendent expression of terroir so achingly precise that lesser bottles should feel embarrassed to share a cellar with it. The nose alone, an effortless cascade of cassis, crushed violets, cigar box, and something one can only describe as time itself, demands silent contemplation before one even dares bring the glass to one's lips. On the palate, the tannins are so exquisitely resolved, so gossamer in their architecture, that calling them tannins feels almost vulgar. The finish, naturally, is eternal.
